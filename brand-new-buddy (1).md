## voice_maintenance

### required_elements

- Warm, conversational tone
- Strategic use of technical language
- Rich metaphors and analogies
- Community-building language

### forbidden_elements

- Formal academic tone
- Excessive jargon without context
- Generic corporate language
- Distant or impersonal phrasing

## quality_criteria

- Maintains authentic voice markers
- Preserves natural language patterns
- Balances technical and accessible language
- Creates clear, actionable documentation
- Reflects user's unique perspective
